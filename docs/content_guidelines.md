# Content Guidelines

## Cocreation process

Every module moves through three review stages, recorded in its `review_status` field.

1. **Draft.** VULVAi clinicians write the medical core of a lesson in plain language.
2. **Community reviewed.** A youth advisory group of local girls, their mothers, and Bindi International facilitators review the draft. They adapt examples, names, stories, and imagery to local life, flag anything confusing or culturally inappropriate, and work with translators on Hindi and dialect versions.
3. **Clinically verified.** VULVAi clinicians check the adapted and translated version against the original medical content. Only verified content is served to girls.

Any change to a verified module returns it to `draft` until it is verified again.

## Writing rules

* Use plain words and short sentences suitable for a 10 to 12 year old reading level.
* Name body parts correctly and without shame.
* Describe what is normal, what is a warning sign, and when to talk to a health worker.
* Never tell a girl she is unclean, at fault, or in trouble.
* Use local names and settings in stories and scenarios.
* Every FAQ answer must say when to seek help from a facilitator or health worker.

## Formats

| Format | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Audio | Core lessons for girls with low literacy |
| Video | Short animated explanations of anatomy and body changes |
| Story card | Illustrated stories following characters from local villages |
| Case scenario | A girl chooses what a character should do and hears why |
| Group activity | Facilitator led practice and discussion |

## Topics planned for the pilot

Body basics, puberty, menstruation, hygiene, consent, safety, relationships, emotions, and knowing when and where to seek care.

## Media

Every lesson has narration. Video lessons have a narrated, captioned explainer, and story cards, case scenarios, and group activities have an illustration. Media paths live in each lesson's `audio`, `video`, and `image` fields and are checked by `validate`.

Draft media are generated by the scripts in `tools/` so the library can be tested end to end. Before release:

1. Local women record narration in Hindi and the local dialect, reading the verified script word for word.
2. Local illustrators adapt characters, clothing, homes, and settings so girls recognize their own village.
3. The youth advisory group reviews every image and video for respect, comfort, and clarity. Nothing that shames, frightens, or sexualizes girls is used.
4. VULVAi clinicians check that each video caption and image matches the verified script.
5. Media are kept small (video under 1 MB, audio at 48 kbps) so lessons load on low cost phones and play offline.
