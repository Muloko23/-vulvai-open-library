# Facilitator Guide

For Bindi International facilitators leading Know My Body sessions in Digital Community Schools.

## Your role

You create a safe space where girls can learn about their bodies without shame. You do not need to be a doctor. The lessons have been checked by clinicians. Your job is to guide, listen, and connect girls to help when they need it.

## Before the program

1. Complete safeguarding training and read `docs/safety.md`.
2. Collect consent from a parent or guardian and assent from each girl.
3. Give each girl a learner code card. Never write her name on the phone or in the app.
4. Make sure shared phones are charged and the app is loaded.

## Weekly session (about 60 minutes)

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 10 minutes | Welcome and ground rules: we listen, we do not laugh, what is shared here stays here |
| 20 minutes | Group plays the week's audio or video lesson together |
| 15 minutes | Story card, case scenario, or group activity, with discussion |
| 10 minutes | Each girl takes the quiz on a shared phone |
| 5 minutes | Reminder that girls can ask private questions in the app or to you at any time |

## When a question is flagged

The app routes questions about abuse, pregnancy, self harm, serious symptoms, or early marriage to you. When you receive one:

1. Talk with the girl privately and calmly within 48 hours, sooner if she may be in danger.
2. Listen. Do not blame her or ask her to prove what she says.
3. Follow the referral pathway: ASHA worker or health centre for health concerns; your Bindi International safeguarding lead and Childline 1098 for safety concerns.
4. Record only the learner code, the date, and the action taken.

## Ground rules for talking about bodies

* Use the correct names for body parts.
* Answer what you know, and say "let's find out together" when you do not.
* Never tell a girl that her period makes her unclean.
* Respect each girl's pace. No girl has to speak in the group.
