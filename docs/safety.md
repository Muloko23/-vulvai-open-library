# Safeguarding and Data Protection

The platform serves adolescent girls, so safety is designed into every layer.

## Safeguarding

* Questions that may signal abuse, pregnancy, self harm, a serious symptom, or early marriage are never answered automatically. The girl receives a supportive message and a trained Bindi International facilitator follows up in person.
* Facilitators complete safeguarding training before the pilot and follow a written referral pathway to local health services and child protection support.
* Safety routing is evaluated against community reviewed test questions in each language before deployment. Any question flagged by either the keyword baseline or the classifier goes to a human.

## Consent

* Parental or guardian consent and the girl's own assent are collected before enrollment, following the approved ethics protocol.
* Girls may stop at any time without consequence.

## Data protection

* No names, phone numbers, or precise locations are stored for girls. Each girl has a pseudonymous ID.
* Data stored: quiz scores, module completion, and records of flagged questions for facilitator follow up.
* Study data are analyzed only in aggregate and anonymized form.
* Data handling follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and UNICEF's guidance on children's data.
